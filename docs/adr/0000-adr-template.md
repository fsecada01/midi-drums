# NNNN. Title of the decision

> **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md) | Deprecated
> **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem or force made this decision necessary? What constraints applied?
Keep this factual and neutral — describe the situation, not the chosen fix.

## Decision

What was decided, stated as a plain declarative sentence, followed by the
key details needed to act on it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include accepted trade-offs and
known limitations, not just upside — a decision record that only lists
benefits isn't trustworthy.

## References

Links to source design docs (if migrated from one), the PR/issue that
implemented the decision, and related ADRs.
